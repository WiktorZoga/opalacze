# BRANDROT: turn any brand into brainrot

1. Write a **brand seed genome** with the geneticist: hero = the brand's
   mascot or most recognisable object, setting = where customers meet it,
   catchphrase = the tagline said wrong. Dominance of `hero` ≥ 0.9.
2. Ask the scout for **one** trend with velocity ≥ 2 whose `structure`
   gene fits a 6–10 s loop.
3. Run the normal loop. The brand seed is always the mother, the trend the
   father, so the brand survives every crossover.
4. Critic threshold for brand mode: `brandSafe` ≥ 8.
5. End with a one-paragraph client brief: the meme, why it fits the brand,
   the post plan, and the fitness target for the next generation.
